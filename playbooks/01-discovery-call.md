# Discovery Call Guide

**Goal:** leave the first call with a real problem, a named owner, a number that would prove success, and access to the data.
**Length:** 45–60 minutes. **Ratio:** they talk ~80% of the time.

Grounded in *The Mom Test*: ask about past behaviour and specifics, never about hypothetical futures or your idea.

## Before the call

- [ ] Read their public material: annual report, org chart, recent press, job posts (these show what they are struggling to staff).
- [ ] Write down your three best guesses at the problem, so you can notice when you are wrong.
- [ ] Know who is on the call: the **economic owner** (pays), the **operator** (does the work today) and the **gatekeeper** (IT, security, data).

## Agenda

### 1. Current workflow (15 min)
- "Walk me through the last time you did X, step by step."
- "Which systems did you touch? Where did the data come from?"
- "Where did it slow down or go wrong?"
- "Who else was involved? Who signs off?"

### 2. Cost of the problem (10 min)
- "How often does this happen?"
- "What does it cost when it goes wrong — hours, money, risk, customers?"
- "What have you already tried? Why didn't it stick?"
- "If this were solved tomorrow, what would you do with the time or money?"

### 3. Success and constraints (10 min)
- "In 6 weeks, what would you need to see to call this a success?" → push for **one number**.
- "What cannot change?" (systems, compliance, unions, budgets)
- "Where does the data live, and who can give us access?"
- "Are there security or approval steps we should start now?"

### 4. Close (5 min)
- Repeat back the problem in one sentence and get a "yes, exactly".
- Agree the next step, **with a date and an owner**: data access, a site visit, a follow-up with the operator.

## Red flags
- Nobody can name the owner or the number.
- "We want to use AI" with no workflow attached.
- Data access "will be sorted later".
- The operator who does the work is not in the room and never will be.

## After the call (same day)
Write up the call using the [Scoping Doc](02-scoping-doc.md) and send the one-sentence problem statement back for confirmation.
