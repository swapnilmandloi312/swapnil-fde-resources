# Weekly Customer Update — Template

> Send every Friday. Answer first (Pyramid Principle). Readable in 60 seconds. Bad news goes in the first line, not the last.

**Subject:** [Project] Week N — 🟢 On track / 🟡 At risk / 🔴 Off track

**Bottom line:** _One sentence: where we are against the success metric and whether the date holds._

**Metric:** _Baseline → current → target_ (e.g. 12 h/week → 7 h/week → 3 h/week)

**Done this week**
- _Outcome, not activity: "Analysts now reconcile vendor invoices in the tool" — not "worked on reconciliation"._
